1. A Clear Morning!
At 7:30 a.m., Anna opened the window and saw bright sunshine. "What a
beautiful day!" she said. The temperature was 22 degrees, and a light breeze
moved the leaves outside.

2. The Shopping List:
- 3 apples
- 1 loaf of bread
- 2 bottles of water
- 500 grams of rice
The total came to $18.75—not bad at all!

3. A Small Project
Ben had 4 weeks to build a garden bench. He measured the wood: 120 cm by
30 cm. Then he checked the plan twice (measure twice, cut once!) and started
working. By Friday, the bench was ready: strong, neat, and useful.

4. At the Library
Mia borrowed 5 books: 2 mysteries, 1 history book, and 2 adventure stories.
The librarian said, "Please return them by June 14." Mia replied, "Of course!
I won't forget."

5. A Quick Trip
The train leaves at 9:45 a.m. from Platform 6. A one-way ticket costs $12.50;
a return ticket costs $21.00. Please arrive 10 minutes early and keep your
ticket safe.

6. Useful Notes
Save your work every 15 minutes. Use a strong password with letters, numbers,
and symbols (for example: Blue7!River#). Never share it with anyone.
If something goes wrong, pause, check the instructions, and try again.

7. Weekend Plans
On Saturday, we will visit the park, walk 2 km, and have lunch at noon. If it
rains, we can watch a movie instead. Either way, the plan sounds great!

8. The Recipe
Mix 2 cups of flour, 1 egg, and 3/4 cup of milk. Add a pinch of salt, then
stir for 2 minutes. Cook each pancake for about 1 minute per side. Serve warm
with fruit, honey, or jam. Enjoy!

9. A Friendly Reminder
The meeting is on Monday, March 18, at 10:00 a.m. Please bring your notebook,
3 printed copies, and any questions you would like to ask. See you there!

10. The End
Every day brings a new opportunity to learn, help someone, or try something
different. Take one small step today—and celebrate your progress!
